# Project vision

## CURA AI

**Curated Intelligence. Personalized Insights.**

CURA AI helps people understand what matters instead of merely consuming an endless news feed. It will curate trusted AI and technology information, personalize it to each user, show multiple clearly-labelled perspectives, and enable grounded conversations with the underlying sources.

## Design principles

1. Personal before popular.
2. Multiple perspectives, clearly labelled as AI-generated analysis.
3. Transparent sourcing and clear separation of source material from AI output.
4. Conversation over passive consumption.
5. User control over personalization, privacy, and AI-provider choice.
